About these estimates

This calculator provides a preliminary estimate for educational purposes only. It is not tax, legal, or financial advice and does not guarantee your actual tax liability, refund, filing requirement, or eligibility. Tax results depend on your complete facts and current federal, state, and local law.

This disclaimer explains the limits of an estimate. It does not reduce our responsibilities to you.

How we build them

  • Every rate, threshold, and exclusion comes from official primary sources, recorded with its source and the tax year it applies to.
  • Tax figures are held separately from the calculator code, so an annual update changes one reviewed file rather than being scattered through the site.
  • An assigned tax professional verifies each figure before a calculator is released, and the verification is recorded.
  • Each tool shows its tax year and last-updated date.

Your privacy

  • Calculations run in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to us.
  • Nothing is stored, logged, or shared, and no analytics or advertising tools receive your figures.
  • No calculator asks for a Social Security number, bank details, or document uploads.
  • Nothing you type appears in the page address, so it will not end up in your browser history.
