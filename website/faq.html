FAQ-C-002

Which services are currently planned?

The approved core includes individual returns, Schedule C, Schedule E, S corporation returns, and conditionally available partnership returns.

Which states are currently in the launch plan?

Washington, California, New York, Illinois, Texas, and Florida, with other states listed as coming soon.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing uses a base amount plus additions for forms, states, and complexity. No public final fee is promised before scope review.

Can I upload tax documents now?

No. Personal accounts and secure document upload are not operational yet. Do not send sensitive documents through the public website or regular email.

What if I do not know which form I need?

Start with Find My Service or review the Individual Tax and Business Tax pages.
